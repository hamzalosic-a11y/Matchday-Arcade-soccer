What the game stores

Playing offline (quick matches, career, Dream Team against the computer): your progress is saved only on your own device, in the browser or app storage. We do not receive it.

If you create an account (needed for ranked online play):

  • Your username. It is public (shown on the ranked ladder) and permanent.
  • Your password. We never see it in plain text; it is stored securely by our login provider.
  • An internal login address made from your username. We do not ask for your real email address.
  • Your ranked results: rating, wins, draws, losses and games played.

During an online match, your controls and the game state are sent between you and your opponent so you can play together.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show personalised ads or track you across other sites. [If you later add ads, update this section.]
  • We do not ask for your location, contacts, photos or microphone.

Purchases

If you buy packs, the payment is handled by Stripe on its own checkout page. We never see or store your card details. We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded, the amount, and the account it was for, and we keep that receipt record for accounting even if you later delete your account. Your Dream Team squad is also backed up to our database so your purchases are not lost if you change device.

Who processes the data

Accounts, the ranked ladder and Dream Team backups are run on Supabase (a hosting and database provider), and payments on Stripe. Your data is stored on their servers. [Add your hosting provider, for example Netlify, if you want to name it.]

Your choices

  • Delete your account: open Account in the game and press "Delete my account". This removes your account, username and ranked history.
  • Clear local data: clearing your browser or app storage removes the progress saved on your device.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at the address above and we will delete it.
